Passes

  • Without a pass, the fruit game is free. A pass unlocks every game.
  • Every new player gets one free day pass.
  • Day and week passes don't expire before you use them. Their time starts when you tap Use, and one pass must run out before the next can be used.
  • Month and year passes start when you buy them and renew automatically. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Bought a pass on another device, or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore Purchases on the pass page.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

卡券

  • 没有卡时可以免费玩切水果，有卡可以玩所有游戏。

  • 月卡和年卡购买后立即生效并自动续费。取消方法：打开 iPhone 的“设置”，点你的名字，再点“订阅”。
