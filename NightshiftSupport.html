Passes

  • Without a pass, Fruit Slash is free. A pass unlocks every game.

  • Week passes don't expire before you use them. Their time starts when you tap Use, and one pass must run out before the next can be used.
  • The lifetime pass is bought once and opens every game for good, right away. Nothing renews automatically.
  • Bought a pass on another device, or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore Purchases on the pass page.
